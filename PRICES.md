# Canonical Prices (Level 1)

Level 1 budget is 100. Target window for a built spell is 90 to 100. All prices are simple point values. Damage is priced per die size and die count, never per point of average damage.

## Dice
| Die | Price per die |
|---|---|
| d12 | 30 |
| d10 | 25 |
| d8 | 20 |
| d6 | 15 |
| d4 | 10 |

- Flat +1 costs 5 each (Magic Missile's +1 per dart counts three times).
- Dice that gain HP cost 2x.
- Dice that set an HP pool cost 1/2.
- Bane and Bless dice cost 10 per creature.
- False Life's flat +4 costs 5 per +1.
